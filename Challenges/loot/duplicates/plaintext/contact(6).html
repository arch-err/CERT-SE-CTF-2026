Audience

  • Information for

    National / EU authorities

    ENISA supports National and EU authorities by enhancing cyber policy, ensuring ICT trustworthiness, and building resilience against cyber threats through collaboration and knowledge sharing.

  • Information for

    Private Sector

    ENISA supports the private sector in enhancing cybersecurity, ensuring trustworthy ICT products and services, and fostering resilience in Europe's digital landscape.

  • Information for

    Citizens

    ENISA provides essential cybersecurity advice and resources, promoting digital safety for European citizens. 

ENISA, is the Union's agency dedicated to achieving a high common level of cybersecurity across Europe.
